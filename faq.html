付费前常见问题 FAQ

生效日期：2026-11-27　　最后更新：2026-11-27　|　以下回答基于产品当前真实实现；涉及退款与个案处理的回答保持诚实，不作过度承诺。

Q1：我的数据安全吗？ / Is my data safe?

EN: Is my data safe?

你的记忆与笔记保存在你自己的浏览器 localStorage 中，不会上传到我们的服务器。你可以在设置中启用 Privacy Vault，用 AES-256-GCM（PBKDF2 210,000 次迭代）加密本地记忆，口令只有你自己知道、我们也不保存。请注意：口令丢失无法恢复，清理浏览器数据或更换设备可能导致本地数据丢失，请务必自行备份。详见隐私政策第 3 节。

EN: Your notes live in your own browser's localStorage and are never uploaded to our servers. You can enable Privacy Vault to encrypt local memories with AES-256-GCM (PBKDF2, 210k iterations). Only you know the passphrase — we never store it. Note: a lost passphrase can't be recovered, and clearing browser data or switching devices may erase local notes, so please back up.

Q2：你们能看到我的聊天记录和记忆吗？ / Can you see my chats or notes?

EN: Can you see my chats or notes?

不能。对话与记忆内容保存在你的浏览器本地，我们的服务器只记录匿名埋点事件（clientId、事件类型）和付款认领状态，不存储、不读取你的内容。详见隐私政策第 1 节。

EN: No. Your conversations and notes stay in your browser. Our servers only record anonymous telemetry (clientId, event type) and payment-claim status — never your content.

Q3：为什么要收费？ / Why do you charge?

EN: Why do you charge?

AGI Engine 是独立开发的产品。收费是为了让开发、维护与支持可持续，而不是靠广告或出售用户数据变现。你支付的是软件使用权限；模型调用费用由你与模型服务商直接结算，不包含在内。具体 Pro 权益以付费页面展示为准。

EN: AGI Engine is independently developed. Charging keeps development, maintenance, and support sustainable — without ads or selling user data. You pay for the software license; model usage costs are billed directly by your model provider and are not included. Pro benefits are as shown on the checkout page.

EN: You can cancel anytime, but paid fees are generally non-refundable. If you're double-charged or your service wasn't activated after payment, email 80306495@qq.com to request a refund. Approval, amount, and timing are decided case-by-case by the founder (subject to founder confirmation) — we don't guarantee any specific outcome. You may also file a claim with PayPal; eligibility is decided by PayPal.

Q7：我必须自己提供 API Key 吗？模型费用由谁承担？ / Do I need my own API key, and who pays for model usage?

EN: Do I need my own API key, and who pays for model usage?

是的。本产品不含 AI 模型，你需要在浏览器中设置自己的 API Key，直连你选择的模型服务商（如 OpenAI、Anthropic、OpenRouter、阶跃，或本地 Ollama）。Key 不会上传到我们服务器，模型调用费用由你与服务商直接结算，与 Pro 费用分开。详见服务条款第 2 节。

EN: Yes. AGI Engine doesn't include an AI model. You'll set your own API key in the browser and connect directly to your chosen provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, StepFun, or local Ollama). Your key is never uploaded to our servers, and model costs are billed directly by the provider, separately from Pro.

Q8：换设备或清理浏览器数据，我的记忆会怎样？ / What happens to my notes if I switch devices or clear browser data?

EN: What happens to my notes if I switch devices or clear browser data?

由于记忆保存在浏览器本地，换设备、清理站点数据或使用无痕模式都可能导致本地记忆丢失。请使用产品内置的导出/备份功能自行保存，并妥善保管加密口令——口令丢失我们无法帮你找回数据。

EN: Because notes are stored locally, switching devices, clearing site data, or using private browsing can erase them. Please use the built-in export/backup feature, and keep your encryption passphrase safe — we can't recover data without it.
